# Shared production instructions

## Priorities
Read the individual channel brief alongside these rules. Current user instructions override imported shared-chat suggestions. BUILDMode's planning and evidence discipline informs all three channels; palette, format and niche remain distinct.

## Decisions from our local video work
- 1080 × 1920, vertical 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 MP4. Choose duration to serve the idea; do not force all videos to 60 seconds.
- Approximately 70% visual storytelling / 30% typography. Show ideas through objects, real interfaces, interactions, diagrams and transformations.
- Use relevant supplied photos, PNGs, screenshots, recordings and logos first. Author purposeful SVG/React objects and diagrams when appropriate. Generate cinematic raster assets when the scene needs photography or illustration.
- Assets and PNG folders contain reusable visual elements, not automatic background images. Use a selected element meaningfully as a foreground object, layer, overlay or genuine evidence; omit it when unnecessary. Do not turn unrelated cutouts or illustrations into wallpaper.
- Generated interfaces must not pass as product screenshots. Simulated interaction is illustration, not measured experimental evidence.
- Neutral dark base and intentional channel accents. Avoid repeated blue boxes, generic gradients, excessive neon and decorative floating dashboards.
- **Editorial Motion is a single opt-in exception**, not a new global palette. When a Reel scene explicitly carries `editorial.template: "editorial-motion"`, use the five swatches ivory `#F2EFE7`, white `#FFFEFA`, mint `#DEF4EA`, peach `#FAE7D7` and lilac `#EDE6FE` with functional grey text/borders. No black product cards or yellow highlight in that selected scene. For every other scene, keep the channel's original palette and current motion direction. See `../docs/EDITORIAL-MOTION.md`.
- For editorial scenes, select `illustrated-explainer`, `saas-ui` or `product-comparison` according to narration; preserve existing audio and captions. The upper layout can reserve lower space for a muted imported presenter video. Never make all videos editorial just because they render using Remotion.
- NO screen-filling solid-colour transition masks/wipes. The user rejected this look. Prefer object continuity, shared geometry, camera pushes, tracking, depth and short overlaps. Inspect transition midpoints and avoid overlapping unreadable text.
- Vary layouts, rhythm, emphasis and transitions. Motion should explain rather than decorate. Restrained grain, shadow, blur, reflection and glow.
- Reuse camera, cursor, typing, browser, code, diagram, image-depth, screenshot, counter, comparison and caption components. Use eased cursor paths, focus/click/hover states, typing pauses and relevant motion beats.
- Captions: approximately 2–7 words at once; readable within platform safe zones; move when needed to avoid the subject. Emphasis can use colour, size, weight or underline rather than a default coloured box.
- Audio is deferred for initial setup/previews. Draft timestamps are estimated; never claim transcription or speech synchronization without a recording.
- When voice-over is supplied, transcribe it and obtain sentence/word timestamps. Preserve the unmodified recording; fit visuals, captions and final duration to the audio. Frame = timestamp × 30.
- Render inside the channel's out/ directory. Review exported frames and transition boundaries, then fix cropping, unreadability, repetitive scenes and abrupt camera motion.

## BUILDMode planning instructions applied to the creation workflow
- Turn developments into experiments: give a tool a real task, show the process, then report what happened. Avoid generic AI news, motivational filler, basic prompt lists and repeated top-five lists.
- No fake projects, fabricated outcomes, invented performance statistics or prewritten winners. Keep [RESULT], [TIME], [WINNER] and [FAILURE] until measured.
- Prefer mini-experiments feasible in 15–90 minutes. Avoid multi-day builds unless the potential justifies the effort.
- Start with curiosity in 1–2 seconds. No logo intro, greeting, channel introduction, “in today's video” or long definition.
- Two Reels per day for seven days: fourteen total. BUILDMode: experiment/challenge + build/result. ToolProof: discovery + test. Tomorrow: what-if + future story.
- BUILDMode length guidance: 15–25s simple experiment, 25–40s most videos, 40–60s deeper challenge. Never stretch an idea.
- Approximately 40% easy, 40% medium, 20% hard across the week. Flag PRIORITY and QUICK BUILD when deserved.
- Research primary sources before timely production. Reverify version, availability, pricing, license, platforms, limits and counts on recording day. Imported chat claims are not fresh verification.
- Complete this production contract for each Reel:
- Day
- Reel number
- Content category
- Topic
- Why relevant now
- Why someone stops scrolling
- Primary performance goal
- Recommended duration
- Three hook options
- Selected hook
- Natural voice-over script
- Scene-by-scene storyboard
- Exact on-screen text
- Assets
- Screen recordings
- Screenshots
- PNGs/icons
- Generated Remotion UI
- Animation instructions for every scene
- Sound effects
- Music mood
- Ending
- CTA
- Instagram caption
- TikTok caption
- 3–5 hashtags
- Thumbnail text (maximum four words)
- Difficulty
- Approximate effort
- Experiment required before publication
- Each weekly plan ends with a top-five production order, actual experiment/build list, combined assets and batching plan.
- Reuse components while varying composition enough that videos do not look identical.
- Posting, uploading and account changes are separate from local creation. No publishing automation is configured.
