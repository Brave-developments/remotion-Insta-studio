# BUILDMode-AI / day 6.2

**One Sentence. One Game.**

Can one sentence describe a playable dodge game? Start with a focused brief rather than a sprawling game design.

Keyboard controls, rising difficulty, a high score, and restart. Four clear requirements keep the first build judgeable.

This authored motion study shows falling obstacles and a moving player. It is animation, not recorded model gameplay.

On the playable local fixture, test movement, collision loss, restart reset, and saved high score after refresh.

A model-build experiment needs actual first output and normal-speed gameplay. Do not invent completion time or correction count.

The development fixture is included beside this study. What game would you describe in one sentence?
