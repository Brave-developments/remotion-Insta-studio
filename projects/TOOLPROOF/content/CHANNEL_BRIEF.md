# TOOLPROOF brief

**Positioning:** I test internet tools so you don’t have to.

**Visual identity:** Evidence-first software testing: real app windows, recordings, pricing evidence, timers and comparisons dominate. Acid-lime passes and restrained red failures.

**Pillars:** Useful tool discovery; Objective hands-on tests; Free versus paid; Open-source software; Creator/productivity tools; Honest limitations.

**Cadence:** 2 Reels/day for 7 days; 14 planned.

Real experiments and test evidence are the substance. Do not render placeholders as factual outcomes. Verify access and functionality; show failures and limitations as well as successes.
