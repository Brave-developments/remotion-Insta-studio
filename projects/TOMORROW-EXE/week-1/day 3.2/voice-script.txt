You do not own a robot. You rent one for two hours.

At nine, a van arrives. A machine steps out and waits for permission to enter.

You point at the sofa. It scans the room, protects the doorway, and moves the heavy end.

For the first hour, this feels like renting a tool. Then you ask it to stay for another job.

The robot cannot. Someone across town has already booked the next slot.

The van leaves. Your furniture moved, and physical labour started looking like cloud computing. Would you let it in?
