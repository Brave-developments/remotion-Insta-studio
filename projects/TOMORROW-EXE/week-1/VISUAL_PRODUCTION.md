Every future event and product interface is an illustration, not experimental evidence or a screenshot of an available product. Factual narration remains the previously researched background; the medical scenes do not portray unrestricted mind-reading, and watermarking is not presented as live caller authentication.

Existing PNG assets were not used as wallpaper. The scenes use native layered objects and environments. The older external asset manifest is an optional photographic/capture replacement queue; those media paths are not required by the implemented vector treatment and must not be interpreted as existing files.

The six beats of each Reel are mapped separately. People, room geometry, the robot, phones and other shared objects preserve continuity; scenes use object motion and camera overlaps rather than solid-color wipes. Estimated captions require measured speech timing if voice-over is supplied later.

TypeScript validation passed after implementation. Final exports and contact-sheet verification are coordinated in the parent production batch.
