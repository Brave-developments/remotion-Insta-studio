# day 1 · AI Gets Your Wallet

Channel: TOMORROW-EXE. Composition: D01R1. Estimated silent-film length: 30s.

## Render edition narration

What happens when your AI gets a spending limit? Payment companies are already building infrastructure for purchases made through AI agents. Now imagine groceries ordered while you sleep, and a flight booked before the fare changes. You set the budget. It decides which purchase deserves it. Then it buys something you never would. The receipt says approved. By whom? Would you give an AI a thousand-dollar limit?
