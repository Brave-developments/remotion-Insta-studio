## Render edition narration

What if your teacher remembered every mistake you made? AI tutors already offer guided learning. Now imagine one that stays with you for ten years. It knows which explanation finally worked, and which question you were too embarrassed to ask. Lessons become personal. So does the record of your confusion, attention, and confidence. When you change schools, does that history follow you? And who gets to read it? An AI might help you learn. Who should own the story of how you learned?
