# day 5.2 · Your Brain Gets a Privacy Policy

## Render edition narration

It is 2038. Your insurance app asks for brain data. Sharing is optional. So is the discount beside the button. At work, a second app offers focus analytics. It says the team will only see averages. You open the policy. The message you intended to send is only one part of the data being collected. You can refuse. But refusing now has a cost attached to it. The interface made communication easier. Would you trade neural privacy for a discount?
