# TOMORROW-EXE / day 4.2

**The Classroom of 2033**

It is 2033. One classroom. Twenty-eight AI tutors.

One student learns fractions with a pizza. Another gets a football diagram. A third needs the problem read aloud.

The teacher stops repeating the explanation and starts noticing who has gone quiet.

A tutor recommends the easier course. The student asks whether that means they are not good enough.

The teacher closes the suggestion. Some decisions need more than a prediction.

If every child gets an AI tutor, what should it never decide for them?
