# TOMORROW-EXE / day 2.2

**The Internet Leaves Your Phone**

It is 2031. Your phone is still at home.

Your glasses place directions on the pavement. The bus time floats above the stop.

At lunch, the menu translates before you finish reading. Technology has become almost invisible.

Then someone says hello. A name appears, followed by where you met and what you discussed.

A permission asks whether you want to remember every conversation. You notice there is no permission for everyone else.

You can finally put your phone down. Would you turn People Memory on?
