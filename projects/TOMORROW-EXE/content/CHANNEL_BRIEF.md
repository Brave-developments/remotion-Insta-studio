# TOMORROW.EXE brief

**Positioning:** Stories from the future.

**Visual identity:** Cinematic near-future documentary: photographs, symbolic objects, phones, maps, city environments and restrained depth. Violet for technology, magenta for consequences, cold blue for factual grounding.

**Pillars:** Work and money; Robotics and daily life; Education; Identity and privacy; Transport and cities; Relationships and synthetic media.

**Cadence:** 2 Reels/day for 7 days; 14 planned.

Clearly frame fiction with Imagine, What if, It’s 203X or This could happen. Separate factual inspiration from speculative events. No fake genuine-looking news or research statistics. Mix optimism, useful ideas, strange scenarios and unsettling questions. REAL TODAY requires verified evidence.
